# 2024 in review: a year of disciplined growth

Revenue grew 31% while operating costs rose only 12%. We ended the year profitable, with more customers, a broader product and a stronger balance sheet.

| Revenue | Gross margin | Customers | Net retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $48.2M | 71% | 2,140 | 118% |

## Letter from the CEO

Two years ago we made a deliberate choice to grow more slowly and build more carefully. In 2024 that choice paid off. We signed our largest customers to date, shipped the grid-forecasting product that had been on our roadmap since the company was founded, and did both without raising new capital.

The numbers in this report describe a company that is larger than it was a year ago. What they cannot show is that it is also calmer. Teams plan in quarters instead of weeks. Our incident count fell by half. People take their holidays.

> We stopped asking how fast we could grow and started asking how long we could keep growing. The second question turned out to have a much better answer.
>
> — Amara Okafor, Chief Executive

I am grateful to the customers who pushed us, to the team who delivered, and to the board for its patience.
